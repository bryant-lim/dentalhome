About Dental Home

Welcome to Dental Home, your trusted dental clinic in Puchong and Bukit Jalil. We believe that dental care should be a comfortable and friendly experience, where you can laugh, share stories, and build trust with your dentist.

Our team of friendly dentists is committed to providing you with excellent dental care that makes you feel like a part of our family. We take pride in putting a big smile on our patients’ faces, which is our greatest motivation.

At our clinic, we want you to feel right at home. Come visit us and experience the care you deserve. Let us take care of your dental needs and help you achieve a healthy and beautiful smile.

Scaling & Polishing

Teeth Whitening

Braces
